Registered is not the same as operating

A registered office is a legal address, not a place of work. Formation agents and virtual offices host hundreds of companies at one address, and many registered companies are dormant or being struck off. "Operating" excludes all three wherever the register publishes the signal (– = not published).

Where fishing really matters

Location quotient = a town's share of fishing companies ÷ its country's share. 10 means fishing is ten times more prominent there than nationally, so small fishing ports rank above big cities with a few fishing firms.
